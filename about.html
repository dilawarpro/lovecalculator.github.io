About LoveCalcu

A simple love calculator for fun

LoveCalcu is a free, name-based love calculator created by Dilawar Khan. Enter two names to get a playful compatibility percentage—no account, sign-up, or birth date required.

The result is generated in your browser from the names and current date. It is intended as a lighthearted icebreaker, not as a scientific measure of compatibility, relationship advice, or a prediction about the future.
