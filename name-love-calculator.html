The quick guide

How does the name love calculator work?

A name love calculator takes two names and turns them into one playful percentage. On LoveCalcu, the score is always between 30% and 100%, and it comes only from the letters and numbers in the names you type.

There are no birthdays, zodiac signs or personal questions. Your name match is created in your browser, and it is meant for fun, not as a scientific test or a prediction.

From two names to one score

  • Tidy. Both names are lowercased, and spaces and punctuation are removed.
  • Sorted. The names are placed in alphabetical order, so you can type them either way round.
  • Mixed. A fixed formula turns the letters into one whole number from 30 to 100. That is your name match.
  • Four bars. Trust, chemistry, friendship and communication are worked out from the same names and kept close to your main score.

Who can you check with a name love calculator?

What can change your name match score?

  • Spelling and accents. Jose and José give different scores.
  • Nicknames and full names. Alex and Alexander are different names to the calculator.
  • Spaces and capitals. They are ignored, so “Mary Jane” and “MaryJane” match.
  • Numbers and other alphabets. Numbers and letters from any alphabet count. Emoji and punctuation are ignored.

Privacy: what happens to the names you type

Name love calculator FAQs

What is a name love calculator?

It is a fun tool that turns two names into a match percentage. It only looks at the letters and numbers you type, so it is for entertainment and not a real compatibility test.

Should I enter first names or full names?

Either works. The calculator scores exactly what you type, so first names and full names give different results. Use the names you like best and keep them the same when you compare scores.

Can I use nicknames in the name love calculator?

Yes. A nickname counts as a different name, so Alex and Alexander give different scores.

Does the order of the two names matter?

No. The two names are put in alphabetical order before scoring, so Alex + Sam and Sam + Alex both give 62%.

Do I need a birth date or zodiac sign?

No. This name love calculator only needs two names. There is no birth date, zodiac sign or sign-up.

Is the name match score accurate?

No. It is a playful number made from spelling alone and cannot measure real feelings or whether two people are right for each other.

Who can I check with the name love calculator?

Anyone you like: a crush, a partner, a best friend, a sibling, a celebrity couple or characters from a book, show or film.

How long can the names be?

Each name can be up to 30 characters. Capital letters, spaces and punctuation are ignored.

Are the names I type saved?

Names are scored in your browser, not on our server. The last four pairs you try are kept in your browser's local storage so you can reopen them, and a shared result link contains both names. See our Privacy Policy for details.
