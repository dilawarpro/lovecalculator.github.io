The quick guide

How does the crush calculator work?

The LoveCalcu crush calculator takes your name and your crush's name and turns the letters into a number between 30% and 100%. Capital letters, spaces and the order of the names don't matter.

It cannot read minds or feelings. The score is created in your browser from spelling alone, so it is a fun way to start a smile or a chat, not a way to find out whether your crush likes you.

What happens to the two names

  • Tidy. Both names are lowercased, and spaces and punctuation are removed.
  • Sorted. The names are put in alphabetical order, so the order you type them never changes the result.
  • Mixed. A fixed formula turns the letters into one whole number from 30 to 100. That is your crush match.
  • Four bars. Trust, chemistry, friendship and communication come from the same names and stay close to your main score.

6 kind ways to get to know your crush

  • Start small. A friendly hello or a question about their day is enough.
  • Find common ground. Ask about shows, music, food or hobbies you might share.
  • Listen more than you talk. People notice when you remember what they said.
  • Be yourself. Real interest is more attractive than trying too hard.
  • Respect their space. If they seem uninterested, stay kind and give them room.
  • Keep the score in perspective. Share the result as a joke, not as pressure.

What can change your crush score?

  • Spelling and accents. Jose and José give different scores.
  • Nicknames. Alex and Alexander are different names to the calculator.
  • Spaces and capitals. They are ignored, so “Mary Jane” and “MaryJane” match.
  • Identical names. Typing the same name twice does not give 100%. Alex + Alex scores 33%.
  • Numbers and other alphabets. Numbers and letters from any alphabet count. Emoji and punctuation are ignored.

Privacy: will your crush see the result?

  • Only if you share it. Nobody is told when you check a name.
  • Scoring happens in your browser. Our server does not need your names to work out a result.
  • Recent pairs stay on your device. The last four pairs you try are saved locally so you can reopen them, and you can remove them any time.
  • Shared links contain the names. Anyone who opens a result link can see both names.
  • Analytics and ads. This site uses Google Analytics and Google AdSense. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Crush calculator FAQs

What is a crush calculator?

A crush calculator is a fun tool that turns your name and your crush's name into a match percentage. It only looks at the letters in the names, so it is just for fun.

Can a crush calculator tell me if my crush likes me?

No. It cannot know anyone's feelings. The score comes only from the spelling of two names, so it says nothing about whether your crush likes you.

Is the crush calculator accurate?

No. It is made for entertainment and cannot measure real feelings or compatibility.

What is a good crush score?

A score of 70% or higher is a strong match here, and 50% to 69% is a promising one. A lower score does not mean anything bad. It is still just for fun.

Do I need my crush's birth date or zodiac sign?

No. You only need two names. There is no birth date, zodiac sign or sign-up.

Does the order of the names matter?

No. The two names are put in alphabetical order before scoring, so Alex + Sam and Sam + Alex both give 62%.

Should I use a nickname for my crush?

You can. A nickname counts as a different name, so Alex and Alexander give different scores. Try both for fun.

Will my crush see my result?

Only if you share it. Names are scored in your browser, but a shared result link contains both names, so anyone who opens it can see them.

Are the names I type saved?

Names are scored in your browser, not on our server. The last four pairs you try are kept in your browser's local storage so you can reopen them, and you can remove them any time. See our Privacy Policy for details.
