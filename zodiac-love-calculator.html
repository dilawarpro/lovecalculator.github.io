How the zodiac love calculator works

Each of the 12 signs belongs to one of four elements: fire, earth, air, or water. Traditional astrology says some elements flow together and others clash. The calculator starts from that pairing, then adds a small variation for the exact two signs. The same two signs always give the same score, in any order.

Zodiac love calculator FAQs

How does the zodiac love calculator work?

It compares the elements of your two signs (fire, earth, air, water) and adds a small sign-based variation to give a playful percentage.

Which zodiac signs are the best match?

In traditional astrology, fire pairs well with air, and earth pairs well with water. Signs that share an element also get along well.

Is zodiac compatibility scientific?

No. Astrology is not supported by scientific evidence. Use this calculator for fun and conversation.

Does a low score mean we should not date?

No. Real relationships depend on trust, respect, and communication, not on star signs.
