The quick guide

How does this love calculator work?

LoveCalcu is a name-based love calculator. It turns the letters of both names into a number between 30% and 100%. Capital letters, spaces, and the order of the names don't matter, so the same pair always gets the same score.

It doesn't ask for birthdays or zodiac signs. The result is created in your browser and is meant for fun, not as a scientific test or a prediction.

6 ways to build real compatibility

  • Listen first. Repeat back what you heard before you answer.
  • Share small moments. A daily message builds more closeness than rare big gestures.
  • Agree on values early. Family, money, and future plans matter most.
  • Argue kindly. Talk about the problem, not the person.
  • Keep your own life. Friends and hobbies make you a better partner.
  • Be honest, softly. Trust grows when truth comes with care.

Love calculator FAQs

Is the love calculator accurate?

No. It is made for fun and cannot measure real feelings or compatibility.

Can I calculate love compatibility by name?

Yes. Enter two names to get a playful love percentage. No birth date is needed.

Why is my result always the same?

The score depends only on the two names, in any order, so the same pair always gets the same percentage.

What is a good love percentage?

Above 70% is a strong match here, and 50% to 69% is a promising one. It is still just for fun.

Does a low score mean we are not a good match?

No. The score cannot say whether a relationship is healthy or right.

Are the names I enter saved?

Names stay in your browser and are not sent to our servers for calculation. See our Privacy Policy for details about the site and its third-party services.
